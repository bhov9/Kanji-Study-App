# Sources & Copyright — concise app draft

Japanese dictionary, kanji, and frequency information is adapted from JMdict, KANJIDIC2, KRADFILE, JmdictFurigana, Jiten, Shiberu, TUBELEX, and wordfreq. Government reading and school-stage information is adapted from the Agency for Cultural Affairs and MEXT. These sources retain their own attribution and license terms; redistributed source-derived data and modifications must carry the notices and any applicable ShareAlike terms listed in the project’s `research/japanese-data/consolidation/SOURCES.md` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

The app’s explanations, organization, examples, and study cues may be original project work, but that does not change the terms of source-derived records. Free access does not by itself grant redistribution rights. Restricted CEJC, BCCWJ, NINJAL, and VDRJ material is excluded from public data unless its specific terms clearly permit the intended redistribution. Consult the fuller notices for exact source versions, licenses, and transformations.
